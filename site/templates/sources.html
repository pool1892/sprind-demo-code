Provenance

  • film.json: every clip's run and episode by a stable identifier, the control steps shown and the SHA-256 of its video and of every record read to find it; every count with the tables it was computed from; the film files with their hashes. The full provenance, with where each file lies on the machine that ran the simulations, is kept with the run records; film.json carries its SHA-256.
  • story-values.json: every number on the main page, its charts' numbers and the fleet record's tables, each with where it came from, and the SHA-256 of the film data and of the page's text.
  • site-files.json: every file of this site with its size and SHA-256.
