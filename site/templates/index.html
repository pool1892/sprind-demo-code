The clips

Every scene in the film is a recorded run of the simulator, shown as it was recorded. Each row names the run, the episode and the control steps shown (one control step is 50 milliseconds), and links the original video. Where the film shows a re-render, it is one that re-executed the recorded actions and matched every recorded state; the original is kept beside it.
